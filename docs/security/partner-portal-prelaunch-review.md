# Partner portal prelaunch privacy and security review

Review date: 2026-11-20. Baseline: `13bf68060413885a757c8cab884aefba3eb99bcb`, including the bounded corrections in this PR. Reassess these conclusions if the backend data model, reporting filters or authorization behavior changes.

This is a dedicated source-based review of the implemented Partner flow, not a completed managed Codex Security scan or a legal compliance certification. The review covers reservation/QR/quota operations, statistics, staff authorization, private collection rules, retention and the four canonical account-erasure callers. It does not authorize production changes, app publication or feature activation. Execution evidence belongs to the PR's actual test results; the existence of a test is not evidence that it ran.

## Product decision and analytics separation

The accepted product requirement is to show how many offers were redeemed, without showing who used them. The user explicitly permits minimal protected account linkage for reservation ownership and one-use enforcement. That supports the implemented operational redemption-count contract; it does not imply consent to location collection, behavioral analytics or a unique-person metric.

The two reporting domains must remain distinct:

| Domain                      | Inputs and purpose                                                                                                                                           | Reporting boundary                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Offer redemption accounting | A successful online staff-confirmed redemption transaction creates an identity-free outbox contribution. It is not a client-reported click or pass-by event. | Exact delayed counts per company/offer and closed UTC month; company managers see their own company, active administrators may see all. Small totals are not advertised as mathematically anonymous. |
| Optional Partner Insights   | Explicit interaction or location-derived analytics, with their own consent, contributor thresholds, feature flags and retention policy.                      | Existing opt-in and minimum-contributor requirements remain applicable. This review does not establish that a separate collector is ready for activation.                                            |

No consent, location event or identity is inferred merely because an offer was reserved or redeemed. The redemption accounting pipeline does not call the Partner Insights collector. Existing billboard interaction recording is a separate path; this review does not claim that the entire navigation journey has no behavioral events. A separate location/behavioral analytics rollout must verify its own implementation against the retained policy; the operational portal's approval is not approval for that rollout.

## Data map and retention

Expiry values below are eligibility thresholds, not guaranteed physical deletion deadlines. The bounded sweep and provider TTL can lag under backlog or failure.

| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

Applicant gets its own projection; administrators review it.         | Canonical account erasure removes the application and owner binding. Public business records have a separate lifecycle. Local application drafts expire after seven days and exclude logos.                                                                        |
| Staff membership                       | Company ID, staff UID, role, enabled state. Operational permission, never a list of customers.                                                                             | Administrator-managed; canonical erasure removes account-owned memberships. Revocation is re-read by protected staff transactions.                                                                                                                                 |
| Reservation                            | Customer UID, campaign/company binding, immutable displayed terms, status, expiry and operational token/retry references. Backend-only storage.                            | Active ownership while usable; terminal records become eligible for cleanup after 30 days. | QR generation and retry                | Opaque random token hash to reservation binding, expiry, scoped retry ID and original result. No raw QR stored in statistics.                                              | Token usability is server-checked for at most 60 seconds and is revoked when a newer generation is issued. The relevant implementation is in `functions/src/partners/{portal.ts,portal-core.ts,portal-statistics.ts,portal-erasure.ts}` and the partner collection matches in `firebase/firestore.rules`. A plain hash does not turn a UID-derived key into anonymous data. Its privacy boundary is denied direct access, purpose limitation, scoped server projections and the described lifecycle.

## Authorization and abuse controls reviewed

- The callable uses verified Firebase Auth, App Check outside emulators, current account access and server-owned roles. Administrators need both their custom claim and an authoritative active admin/owner role. No client-supplied UID becomes a customer identity or an administrator role.
- Company managers and redeemers are separated. Company membership, enabled state and company status are read in protected transactions. Cross-company staff cannot inspect or redeem another company's token; redeemers do not get business statistics. The administrator staff directory is operational and is not a customer list.
- The PR adds the authoritative admin role to the write transaction for application review, staff membership changes, billboard logo binding and the changed legacy offer-edit/activation handlers. An earlier successful request-level admin check is insufficient after an in-flight demotion. The role document now participates in transaction conflict detection, and rejected writes produce no successful mutation audit. This is a scoped assessment of the changed Partner integration paths, not a repository-wide authorization claim.
- Cloud Storage copying and Firestore binding are not a cross-service atomic transaction. A copy completed before later authorization denial may leave an unreferenced business-logo object; it does not authorize the billboard binding or an audit claiming success. It must not be described as atomic revocation of an external storage operation, and cleanup must never blindly delete a pre-existing shared object.
- Shared quota transactions enforce `reserved + redeemed <= capacity`, current offer/version/window and customer entitlement after relevant reads. Concurrent cancellation, expiry and redemption update the same reservation/quota state. An administrator preview does not bypass paid customer reservation eligibility.
- The QR has 256 random bits, a hash at rest, one active generation and a server-time expiry. Inspection is separate from confirmed redemption. Two concurrent scanners can produce only one successful state transition; retry identifiers return the original receipt rather than consuming a second place.
- Only confirmed server redemption creates an outbox event. Aggregation atomically updates the all-time and monthly totals and replaces the event with a minimal processed marker. Replaying the aggregation does not count a redemption twice.
- Reports are delayed daily snapshots, exclude the current UTC month as a monthly filter and have no customer/unique-person/time-of-day/location slices. Per-offer and per-company totals are still sparse exact counts: daily differences and merchant knowledge can reveal activity in small groups. The API does not promise to prevent that inference.
- Direct reads and writes to private reservations, tokens, quotas, staff bindings, outbox and statistical collections are denied, including administrator client SDK access. New logo input is bounded, decoded/re-encoded PNG with metadata removal; SVG, arbitrary server fetching and animation are excluded.

## Canonical account deletion

All four entrypoints reach `purgeUserData` in `functions/src/account/scheduled.ts`: scheduled requested deletion, inactivity cleanup, administrative deletion and never-onboarded cleanup. Partner erasure runs before the ordinary account tree is removed.

The purge first installs `partnerErasureActive` in shared progress, then drains reservation references and campaign-scoped claims in bounded, resumable work. Relevant partner writes read the deletion request/progress fence transactionally. Missing user records reject an old token after progress removal. An incomplete page or failed commit keeps a retry anchor/progress marker instead of acknowledging completion. The shared RSVP progress writer merges its own keys and cannot overwrite partner progress.

Only reserved capacity is refunded; redeemed totals and the separate business profile remain. Private application contacts, owner pointers and staff bindings are part of the shared purge plan. Existing administrative deletion/proof/audit records have their own pre-existing policy; this review does not promise removal of every administrator audit identity or backup copy. The hashed minute-level anti-abuse residual above also remains explicitly bounded by expiry eligibility rather than immediate canonical enumeration.

## Validation record

The same regression file was executed against the unchanged baseline and the corrected source using real local demo Firebase services. The role interleaving executes the complete real administrator entry guard first, verifies its actual authorized actor, then commits the role demotion before allowing the handler to continue. It retains the verified JWT claim and returned actor; it does not fabricate an authorized result or replace the business transactions. A reached-stage assertion and a real user-document read precede the strict permission-denied assertion, so a rejection before the intended race window cannot count as proof.

- Baseline `13bf68060413885a757c8cab884aefba3eb99bcb`: all seven selected regressions failed for their expected reasons. Six privileged operations completed after the proven demotion, and the processed outbox marker still retained redundant fields. Sixteen unrelated tests in that baseline file were deliberately not selected.
- Corrected source: 42 cases passed with zero failures or skips across `partner-portal.emulator.test.ts` (23), `partner-erasure.emulator.test.ts` (11), `partners.emulator.test.ts` (7) and `partner-portal-transport.emulator.test.ts` (1). These include capacity contention, QR replay, expiry/cancellation, private-rule denial, canonical erasure, legacy offer behavior and an actual local SDK Auth/Functions transport flow.
- The role cases require permission-denied, unchanged domain records and no successful mutation audit. The outbox case requires an exact `{ processed: true }` marker and unchanged redemption totals after repeated publication.

These are local emulator results. They do not replace the PR's CI, physical-device acceptance, a completed managed scan or the separate consent release gate below.

## Limits that remain before activation

- One use means one authenticated account per campaign. Multiple accounts, account recreation after purge, merchant/customer collusion and fabricated physical fulfillment cannot be excluded without additional controls and privacy tradeoffs. No device fingerprint or cross-account tracking is silently introduced.
- A still-live screenshot can be forwarded. Online company-scoped staff authorization, the short generation lifetime and single successful redemption limit replay; they do not prove that the person presenting the screenshot is the original account holder. A merchant can also correlate an operational receipt with a visitor outside the app. The app must promise no customer-identity disclosure in its projections, not that a merchant cannot recognize a visitor.
- Keep the distinction between exact aggregate counts and anonymity in product copy. Do not reuse the optional analytics opt-in for a new purpose or expose finer statistical segmentation without another review.
- Consent verification for existing billboard/Partner Insights recording remains a separate unresolved release gate for the full Partner journey. The new redemption-counter pipeline's assessment does not clear those collection paths or imply analytics consent.
- Verify physical camera scanning, Android/iOS online and offline UI, configured native Mapbox logos and company navigation using actual device evidence. Emulator and web fixture results do not satisfy those physical-device gates.
- Preserve the flag's documented scope: OFF blocks new reservation/profile/offer mutations but does not stop application handling, authorized reads, permitted existing-reservation cancellation/redemption or maintenance schedules. Publication and flag activation remain separately authorized release steps.
- Review actual tests and CI for this PR, including six deterministic admin-demotion interleavings, minimal-marker/replay coverage, capacity contention and canonical erasure. Do not convert source inspection or an unavailable managed scan into a clean-scan guarantee.

Source review conclusion: the operational reporting design can meet the accepted aggregate-only requirement when described with these explicit boundaries. No claim of mathematical anonymity, one-physical-person enforcement, legal certification, completed managed scan or production release approval is made. The bounded role/marker corrections and remaining device/activation gates must be considered together with the PR's actual validation evidence.
