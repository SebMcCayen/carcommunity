# Approved visual references, 2026-10-24

These JPEG pages are review-sized exports of the user-supplied design catalogue,
not screenshots of the running app. People, vehicles, maps, badges, balances,
offers and messages are illustrative. Do not bundle these pages into the app or
seed their content into production. Use real runtime assets and data.
